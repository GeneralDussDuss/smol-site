02 // THE STATES

Four Ways To Be Alive

A soul that only changes volume reads as a screensaver. These change shape, and the same language runs across all three.

· IDLE

Sits back

Smaller, dimmer, a slow breath. Mochi goes half-lidded. Bit goes sparse. The palette drifts.

◌ LISTENING

Leans in

Bigger, spin stops, faces you. Sound comes in: rings contract toward the body under a VU tick ring. Mochi's eyes go wide; Bit snaps to a solid upright grid.

◐ THINKING

Churns

A scan band sweeps the body, sparks orbit fast, the color cools. Mochi looks up and away with thought dots; Bit spins on the flow.

♪ SPEAKING

Pushes out

Sound goes out: waves expand from the rim on the voice, a waveform runs through the center. One soft pulse per phrase, never per syllable. Mochi bounces and lets hearts go.

03 // HOW IT IS DRAWN

Two Draws a Frame

Eight pieces, one sentence each. The spec page has the exact numbers.

01the soul shader

One fragment shader per soul, rendered into a 160 px square (256 on device), taking breath, energy, beat, flow, voice and three state weights.

02the palette

Cyclic, c1 → c2 → c3 → c1, offset by a slow global shift and a second noise field across the body, so the soul drifts through the theme's accents instead of sitting on one hue.

03the cell grid

The soul region is columns and rows of the UI font; a cell is one monospace advance wide and two tall, so braille and half-blocks land where text would.

04braille

Eight sub-dots per cell, sampled at their centers, thresholded against a 2×4 ordered dither, packed into U+2800 plus a mask; the glyph takes the alpha-weighted color of what lit it.

05ramp

Mean luminance into .:-=+*#%@; classic ASCII art and the mid-tier fallback. Mochi's face reads best here.

06blocks

Top and bottom halves sampled separately, posterized to four levels, drawn as a filled cell plus an upper half block. Bit's native look.

07the state machine

Idle, listening, thinking and speaking are eased weights, not switches, so a soul fades from one signature into the next; the launcher drives them from the loop.

08on Android

RuntimeShader one is the soul in AGSL; RuntimeShader two is the glyph pass with the soul as a child shader and a bitmap atlas of 256 braille glyphs. Zero readback. A CPU text path covers the charging screen and budget phones.
