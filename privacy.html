Privacy

smol collects nothing.

Last updated 29 October 2026. This is the whole policy, and it is short on purpose.

What we collect. Nothing. smol has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporter. There is no smol server for your data to reach.

Where your data lives. Everything you do in smol stays on your phone: your commands, notes, reminders, settings, and the audit log of what the assistant proposed and what you decided. The AI model runs on the device. Uninstalling smol removes it all.

Network. smol makes no network requests while you use it. The only network use is the one-time download of the AI model files when you first set up the brain, and no personal data is sent with that download. When a command opens a web page or a map, your own browser or map app does it.

Permissions. smol asks for a permission only when you first use the feature that needs it, and uses it only for that feature. The microphone is opened only while you hold the soul or tap the note glyph, and speech is recognised on the device. Contacts are looked up locally so that "call mom" can find a name; the dialer or messaging app makes the final press. Notification, alarm, brightness and do-not-disturb access serve the reminders, timers and toggles you ask for. None of it is sent anywhere.

Children. smol collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this ever changes, the new policy will be published here before it takes effect, with the date above updated.

Contact. Questions or concerns: github.com/GeneralDussDuss.
