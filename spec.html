01 // THE REFERENCE

Same Strings, Readable

The Android port is a translation, not a reinterpretation. These are the exact shader strings this site runs, plus the state mapping and the glyph pass written down.

GLSL ES 1.0 as written for WebGL. For AGSL: main(float2) returns half4, the band texture becomes a float[32] uniform with a manual lerp, mat2 and atan carry over, and no derivatives are used anywhere, so nothing needs fwidth.

The files are published next to this page under spec/ and dumped from assets/souls.js by the build, so they cannot drift from what you see.
