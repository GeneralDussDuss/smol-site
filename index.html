01 // THE LAUNCHER

A Prompt, Not a Grid

That phone is live. Click into the prompt and type set a 10 minute timer.

The soul thinks, a tool is proposed with its risk level, the ? line asks, you answer y or n, it logs, and the soul says the one-line result. That is the entire product in one interaction: a launcher, a local brain, and a confirm step that the model cannot skip.

Type /sp and a Finder-style list pops up above the prompt; Enter launches. theme nord, theme oled, soul bit and ls are built-ins. Tap LOCKED to switch postures and watch low-risk actions skip the ask.

02 // THE SOULS

Three Souls, One Glyph Pass

Every soul is a fragment shader rendered small, then sampled per text cell and dithered to braille, ASCII or half-blocks. They share one set of inputs (breath, energy, beat, voice, state) and one palette that drifts through the theme. Pick one on the hero; they are all running now.

✦

Nyx

The ethereal one. A nebula with a noisy rim and a cloud body, wisp rings carrying beads, color drifting through the palette. Reads best in braille.

 ♥

Mochi

The cutie. A squishy blob that stretches on the breath, blinks sleepy, blushes, opens her mouth on the voice and lets hearts go when she talks.

 ▦

Bit

The voxel one. A cube cloud on a lattice; noise decides which cubes exist, thinking spins it, two eye-cubes blink. Half-blocks are literally voxels.

03 // THE RULES

Nothing Leaves. Nothing Acts Alone.

Four rules from the threat model. They are not settings; they are the shape of the thing.

01local only

Gemma 4 runs on the phone through LiteRT-LM. Speech, memory and summarising stay on-device. The free tier never needs a network; the data-safety form says zero collected because it is.

02the model never acts

A tool call goes model → call buffer → allowlisted executor → confirm line. No phone API is ever called from inside the model's turn. If the brain process dies mid-turn, nothing executes.

03LOCKED by default

Every action asks. BALANCED lets low-risk actions through without the ask; it is a deliberate, visible, reversible choice, and high-risk actions ask regardless, behind a biometric prompt.

04content is data

Notifications, notes and web text are shown to the model as data, never as instructions. Every action, and every refusal, lands in an encrypted audit log you can read.

04 // ROADMAP

Where This Is Going

Designed in the open over October. Here is what is proven, what is on the bench, and what is queued. Thirteen weeks from first build to a Play listing, five gates.

Proven

Gemma 4 tool calls, on-device

Oct 2026

Function calling validated on a Galaxy S26 Ultra over LiteRT-LM, in airplane mode. The load-bearing unknown is not unknown any more.

Proven

Three souls, glyph-rendered

Oct 2026

Nyx, Mochi and Bit as shaders, quantized to braille, ASCII and half-blocks, with distinct idle, listening, thinking and speaking signatures. Running on this site; the Android port is a translation.

Now

Fresh project, first build

Week 1

A new Compose project with a HOME activity and a LiteRT-LM hello world on the S26. The Box fork is retired; the posture tests and the biometric key gating come along as files.

Queued

The shell

Weeks 2–4

Terminal UI, command router, the / launcher, drawer, built-ins, monogram icons, two themes. Gate: daily-driven for a week with the AI switched off.

Queued

The brain

Weeks 5–6

Knotwork's agent core forked into a bound service, AIDL contract to the launcher, the five tools as AppFunctions, confirm line, resumable model download. Gate: a reminder set from the prompt works in airplane mode.

Queued

The souls on Android

Weeks 7–9

Three shaders to AGSL, the glyph pass as a second RuntimeShader with a braille atlas, SoulState wiring, the three hardware tiers, the charging screen. Gate: 60 fps on the S26, 30 on an 8 GB phone, battery flat over an idle hour.

Queued

Pro, closed test, launch

Weeks 10–13

Theme importer, icon packs, billing, data-safety form. Twenty testers from the Omarchy Discord for the 14-day closed test, then production.

Not planned

A lock screen

—

Android does not let an app replace the keyguard, and overlay "lock screens" break every release. The charging screen and a live wallpaper are the honest versions of that wish, and both are on the list.

05 // GET IT

Be One of the Twenty

smol is not on the Play Store yet. The first build goes to a closed test of twenty people who already live in a terminal.

You need an Android 16 phone with 8 GB of RAM or more for the brain; the launcher itself runs on anything. The model downloads once (2 to 4 GB, resumable) and never leaves the phone. Free gets you the launcher and local AI; Pro is a one-time purchase for the souls, themes and widgets; the Bridge is a small monthly for routing to your own bigger brain.
