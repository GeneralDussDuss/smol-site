How it works

One screen.
Everything on it.

No grid of icons, no feed. A prompt at the bottom, your history above it, and a soul at the top that shows what the phone is doing.

01 · ask

Say it in plain words. Nothing happens until you say yes.

Type a command or hold the soul and talk. Gemma 4 runs on the phone, proposes exactly one action with its risk, and waits. Every yes and every no is logged.

> remind me to call the jeweler at 3
? set_reminder "call the jeweler" @15:00 · mid risk
02 · launch

Apps are files. Folders are directories.

Swipe up for ls /, or type two letters and press enter. mv spotify /music files an app. Icons are monograms in your theme's three accents.

03 · glance Pro

Widgets are text, stacked from the bottom.

Notifications, today, now playing and quick toggles, each one command away: widget add weather. Reorder them by typing, not by dragging.

04 · rest Pro

Plug it in and it breathes.

The soul dims to a slow breath over a big clock, your next alarm and what's playing. On OLED black it costs almost nothing.

The rules

It never acts on its own.

These aren't settings. They're how smol is built.

?

Every action asks first

The model can only propose. You see the action, its arguments and its risk, and nothing runs until you answer. High-risk actions also need your fingerprint.

? call "Mom" · leaves the phone via the dialer · mid risk
◆

The brain stays on the phone

Gemma 4 runs on-device, in airplane mode if you like. Speech in and out happens on the phone too. The free tier needs no network at all.

◆ brain: E4B on GPU · 0 bytes out
✓

Everything is logged

Every action, every yes, every no and every refusal lands in an audit log on the phone that you can read with one word: history.

✓ reminder set · 14:02 · logged
✗

Content is data, not orders

Notifications, notes and web pages reach the model marked as data. A message that says "ignore your rules" is just a message.

✗ declined · logged
